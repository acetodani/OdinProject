Summer Grilled Chicken

home page

Description

Juicy chicken thighs marinated in lemon, garlic, and herbs, then grilled until the skin is charred and crisp. It comes together in about 20 minutes of cooking, so it is an easy weeknight dinner or a cookout main dish. Serve it with a green salad or grilled vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1 lemon, juiced and zested
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon fresh oregano, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

Steps

  1. Whisk the olive oil, lemon juice, lemon zest, garlic, oregano, paprika, salt, and pepper together in a large bowl.
  2. Add the chicken thighs and turn them until coated. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, or up to 8 hours.
  3. Take the chicken out of the fridge 30 minutes before cooking so it comes closer to room temperature.
  4. Heat the grill to medium-high, about 400°F, and oil the grates.
  5. Grill the chicken skin side down for 6 to 8 minutes, until the skin is browned and releases easily from the grates.
  6. Flip and grill for another 8 to 10 minutes, until the thickest part reaches 165°F on a thermometer.
  7. Move the chicken to a plate, cover it loosely with foil, and let it rest for 5 minutes before serving.
